# Privacy Policy — Mai (Discord Bot)

Last updated: 2026-11-03

Mai ("the Bot") is a multi-purpose bot for an Albion Online guild community, operated by an individual developer.

## Data the Bot accesses

- **Message content**: read in channels the Bot can access to operate text ("!") commands, sequential message-based flows (such as trade coordination steps that read an amount or a payment link a user posts), and the reaction-favorites feature (the Bot DMs a message's content to a user who reacted to it). Message content is processed in real time and is **not archived**.
- **Server member information**: user IDs, usernames, and member objects, used by the economy system (balances and transfers), moderation tools, and invite tracking.

## Data the Bot stores

Stored in local files on the developer's machine, only to provide the Bot's features:

- Discord user IDs with in-bot virtual currency balances
- Tip totals and VIP rank progress
- Records that users explicitly register with the Bot
- Per-server functional settings

## What the Bot does not do

- The Bot does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
- The Bot does not build message archives or advertising profiles.
- The Bot does not use the Presence intent (not requested).

## Data removal

Users and server administrators may request deletion of their stored data by contacting the developer. Removing the Bot from a server deletes that server's configuration.
